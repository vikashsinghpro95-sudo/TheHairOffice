Lead Practitioners

Your procedure is personally planned and overseen by our founding directors, ensuring the highest medical standards.

Aesthetics Doctor

Dr. Anju Mehta

MBBS, MD (Anaesthesia), JCCP Level 7

Dr Anju Mehta brings over two decades of clinical experience as an Anaesthetist, with a background spanning the NHS and international practice. She transitioned into aesthetic medicine following completion of the Ofqual-regulated, JCCP-approved Level 7 postgraduate diploma in injectables - the highest recognised standard of training in the UK.

She has since undertaken advanced training in regenerative aesthetics and medical hair restoration, including PRP, polynucleotides and evidence-led approaches, allowing her to take a more comprehensive, diagnosis-driven view of hair and scalp health.

At The Hair Office, her work is defined by clinical rigour, precision, and a deep understanding of the biological processes that underpin hair loss. Each treatment plan is highly personalised and ethically considered — with a focus on long-term improvement rather than short-term fixes. Known for her calm and meticulous approach, Dr Mehta builds enduring patient relationships, guiding each individual through a structured, carefully considered treatment journey.

Scalp Specialist

Kelly Morrell

Hair Restoration Specialist

Kelly Morrell brings over 13 years of experience as a specialist in hair restoration, with a focused expertise in advanced, non-surgical treatments for hair loss and scalp health.

Her work is shaped by a detailed understanding of hair biology and regenerative processes, supported by extensive experience in treatments such as growth factor therapies, microneedling, and scalp micropigmentation. She has also incorporated advanced diagnostic approaches, including genetic testing and trichoscopy, to better understand the underlying drivers of hair loss.

At The Hair Office, Kelly's approach is precise, methodical, and highly individualised - centred on identifying the underlying problem before selecting the treatment. Each plan is tailored with care, combining clinical insight with a strong aesthetic sensibility to achieve natural, long-term outcomes. Her work is guided by consistency, clarity, and a commitment to long-term hair health.
