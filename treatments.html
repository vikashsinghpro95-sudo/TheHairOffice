Regenerative solutions for hair loss.

From regenerative therapies to medical care, every treatment plan is tailored after a medical assessment of your scalp, hair cycle and lifestyle.

REGENERATIVE

PRP / PRF

Concentrated platelet therapies that deliver growth factors to the scalp to support follicle recovery and improve hair density.

REGENERATIVE

Polynucleotides

DNA-derived regenerative molecules used to improve scalp quality, reduce inflammation, and support stronger follicle function.

REGENERATIVE

Calecim

Growth-factor rich topical regenerative therapy used after scalp procedures to stimulate follicle activity and improve recovery.

AESTHETIC

SMP

Scalp micropigmentation creates natural follicle-like impressions to restore the appearance of density and hairline definition.

DIAGNOSTIC

Tricho test

Genetic and medical analysis to personalise treatment planning based on individual predisposition and biological response.

DIAGNOSTIC

Needle free instant Blood test

Rapid blood biomarker screening without venepuncture to support safe, personalised, and medically informed treatment decisions.

TRICHOLOGY

Cera Cleanse

Professional scalp detox protocol to remove buildup, rebalance sebum, and optimise scalp conditions for healthy hair growth.

AESTHETIC

Hair cocktail

Targeted blend of nutrients and active compounds delivered to the scalp to nourish follicles and support growth cycles.

LED THERAPY

Red light

Low-level red light therapy helps stimulate follicle metabolism, improve scalp circulation, and support ongoing hair maintenance.
