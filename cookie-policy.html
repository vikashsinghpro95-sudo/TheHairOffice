1. INTRODUCTION

This Cookie Policy explains how The Hair Office Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on www.thehairoffice.com.

This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. Cookies help websites function correctly, remember preferences and provide information about website usage.

3. HOW WE USE COOKIES

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure website functionality
  • Improve user experience
  • Understand website performance
  • Measure advertising effectiveness
  • Maintain security and website operation

4. COOKIE CATEGORIES

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Required for the operation, security and functionality of the website.

Functional Cookies

Used to remember user preferences and improve website functionality.

Analytics Cookies

Used to understand how visitors use the website and to improve website performance.

Marketing Cookies

Used to measure advertising effectiveness and improve marketing relevance.

5. GOOGLE ANALYTICS

The Hair Office may use Google Analytics to collect information regarding website usage. Information may include device information, browser information, pages visited and general website interaction data.

6. META PIXEL

The Hair Office may use Meta Pixel technologies to measure advertising effectiveness, website engagement and audience insights.

7. COOKIE CONSENT

The Hair Office uses CookieYes to obtain and manage cookie consent preferences. Visitors may accept, reject or customise non-essential cookie categories.

8. MANAGING COOKIES

You may manage cookie preferences through:

  • CookieYes settings
  • Browser settings
  • Device privacy settings

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

9. THIRD-PARTY COOKIES

Some cookies may be placed by third-party providers including analytics, advertising or embedded content providers. Such providers are responsible for their own privacy practices.
